Services

Commercial property documentation without the extra trip.

01

Exterior Property Condition Walk

We follow an agreed exterior route, take dated photos, enter short factual notes, and return a professional report for manager review.

Typical agreed areas may include parking lot, drive lanes, entrances, building sides, rear, exterior lighting, drainage areas, dumpster area, sidewalks, and accessible common areas.

02

Interior Property Condition Walk

Accessible commercial interior areas are documented to a route established before the visit.

03

Exterior + Interior

One visit covering the agreed exterior and accessible interior areas, with one organized report.

Minor items: if an eligible minor item is noted, On-Site Property Support may provide a separate written proposal. Nothing is performed automatically during the walk. Work begins only after written approval and only when the task stays within our allowed scope.
